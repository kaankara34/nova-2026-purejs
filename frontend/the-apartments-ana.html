A PRIVATE 3+1 RESIDENTIAL ADDRESS ON KEMAL SUNAL STREET, SHAPED AROUND THE QUIETER SIDE OF LIFE NEAR BAĞDAT CADDESİ.

Ana brings together the peaceful, family-oriented character of Kemal Sunal Street with the everyday advantages of Caddebostan. With Büyük Kulüp just a few steps away and Bağdat Caddesi moments from home, it offers a composed residential setting where neighbourhood calm, privacy and everyday convenience come naturally together.

ARCHITECTURE

A distinctive form, composed for its setting.

The architecture combines clear proportions, generous glazing and a restrained material palette with a strong vertical expression. Contrasting façade elements give the building its identity, while the overall composition remains measured and residential in character. Set within the calm surroundings of Kemal Sunal Street, Ana is conceived as a contemporary addition naturally integrated into its neighbourhood.

Engineered for Structural Safety

The structural design of The Apartments Ana has been reviewed by our structural consultant, Prof. Dr. Cem Yalçın. Following a project-specific structural assessment, Prof. Dr. Yalçın reviewed the structural system, design assumptions and engineering approach, and provided his formal engineering approval for the adopted structural design. The project has therefore been subject to an additional level of technical review beyond the standard design and approval process.
