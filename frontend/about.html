Discover Nova Konut

Nova develops distinguished residential projects defined by architectural integrity, engineering excellence, and uncompromising quality. With a disciplined approach to design, construction, and every technical detail, we create enduring residences that meet the demands of contemporary living while delivering lasting value for generations.

Founded in Istanbul in 2000, Nova has been committed to developing high-quality residential projects that contribute to Türkiye’s economic growth and strengthen its presence in an increasingly global market.

Across every investment, customer satisfaction remains a fundamental priority. Our developments combine respect for the environment with regulatory compliance, advanced technologies, and rigorous standards of quality. Bringing together safety, architectural integrity, aesthetics, and functionality, each project is conceived not only for the needs of today, but with a long-term perspective on how people will live in the future.

Through our continued investments in construction, architecture, and urban transformation, Nova aims to create lasting value, generate employment, and contribute meaningfully to the sustainable development of Türkiye.

Build Beyond Living

Beyond the spaces we create, we shape a distinctive way of living — one defined by design, comfort, culture, and a refined sense of place. Every Nova residence is conceived not simply as a home, but as the setting for a lifestyle with its own character. For us, building goes beyond creating places to live. It is about elevating everyday life, shaping meaningful experiences, and creating enduring value for generations.

SUSTAINABILITY

Certified for sustainable living.

Nova residences are conceived in alignment with LEED standards, combining environmental responsibility with lasting comfort, efficiency, and value.

Building forGenerations

The vision of Nova Konut is to create enduring residential developments where architectural integrity, engineering excellence, and quality come together. Guided by a long-term perspective, each development is conceived to respond to contemporary living while preserving its value, relevance, and architectural character across generations.

Engineered To Endure

Nova projects are shaped by rigorous engineering principles and established technical standards. Structural design and construction are developed in accordance with the Turkish Building Earthquake Code (TBDY 2018) and relevant Turkish and European standards, including TS 500 for reinforced concrete design, TS 498 for structural loads, TS EN 206 and TS 13515 for concrete performance, and TS 708 for reinforcing steel. From structural analysis and material selection to construction methodology and quality control, technical precision remains integral throughout the entire process — ensuring structural integrity, safety, durability, and long-term performance.

Built On Trust

Project delivery marks the beginning of an enduring relationship with our residents. Guided by a principle of unconditional customer satisfaction, Nova remains a trusted solution partner — addressing every concern with accountability, care, and a commitment to lasting resolution.
