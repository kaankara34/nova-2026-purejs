DISCOVER NOVA KONUT: RESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT ON İSTANBUL'S ANATOLIAN SIDE

Nova Konut develops residential projects on the Anatolian side of İstanbul, along and around Bağdat Caddesi in Kadıköy. Each development is delivered with its own architectural identity, considered material selection and quality-controlled construction.

The current portfolio includes The Residences East West in Çiftehavuzlar, The Apartments Taç in Selamiçeşme and The Apartments Ana in Göztepe, alongside completed commercial and institutional work.

Design, construction and sustainability are managed in-house, which keeps architectural intent, build quality and long-term value aligned from the first drawing to handover.
