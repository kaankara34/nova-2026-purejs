RESERVE A RESIDENCE VIEW FLOOR PLAN

Secure 3+1, 4+1 and duplex residences in Selamiçeşme, Bağdat Caddesi, with 190 m² gross and 149 m² net living space.

Taç offers generous rooms and practical layouts designed for spacious living. Set one plot back from Bağdat Caddesi, the development is close to the avenue while sheltered from its bustle and noise. Its open outlook and separation from neighbouring buildings extend the sense of space beyond the interiors.

ARCHITECTURE

Understated, balanced residential architecture.

Dark façade surfaces, generous glazed openings and horizontal lines repeated across the floors define Taç’s architectural character. A light-coloured frame around the upper façade incorporates Nova’s signature V motif, giving the building a distinctive identity. Glass balcony balustrades and warm-toned balcony ceilings complete the composition.
