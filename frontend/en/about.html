Nova Konut

Nova Konut is a real estate investment and construction company focused on premium residential development. Architecture, engineering and construction are managed as one continuous process, with quality, technical competence and long-term value guiding each stage. The Nova signature is defined by a strong architectural identity, exacting construction standards and enduring value.

Founded in Istanbul in 2000, Nova is an investment and development company active in real estate development, construction and urban regeneration. The company is focused on delivering high-quality projects, creating long-term value and strengthening its position in international markets.

Nova’s approach is founded on rigorous quality standards, technical capability, regulatory compliance and customer satisfaction. Architectural design, engineering, construction quality, functionality and environmental responsibility are considered throughout each development. Advanced technologies and contemporary building solutions support safety, efficiency and quality of life.

Through its investments, Nova seeks to create high-quality living environments, add lasting value to the areas in which it operates, support employment and contribute to Türkiye’s long-term sustainable economic development.

Build Beyond Living

Nova treats residential development as an integrated living environment, with architecture, engineering, technology and service infrastructure planned around comfort, efficiency and sustainability. Acoustic performance, circadian lighting, advanced ventilation, indoor air quality, responsible material selection, and energy and resource efficiency are incorporated into the development framework. IoT infrastructure, intelligent automation and AI-assisted management tools support building performance, security and resident experience. A digital service ecosystem extends this approach into day-to-day operations. Concierge services, technical support requests, common-area and amenity reservations, building management and selected lifestyle services can be managed through a single digital platform. Nova Membership carries this service model beyond individual developments, creating continuity across the resident experience. Build Beyond Living defines Nova’s approach to residential development: architecture, engineering, technology and service designed to work as one.

SUSTAINABILITY

Developed in line with sustainability standards.

Nova residences are developed in line with LEED criteria, with environmental performance, resource efficiency, quality of life and long-term value considered within a single development framework.

Enduring Value,Long-Term Perspective

Real estate development is approached with a long-term view of value creation. Regional dynamics, evolving living needs and investment potential are assessed together, with a focus on projects that retain their quality, functionality and economic value over time.

Engineering Standards

Nova developments are delivered in accordance with established engineering principles and relevant technical standards. Structural design and construction processes follow the Turkish Building Earthquake Code (TBDY 2018) and applicable Turkish and European standards. These include TS 500 for reinforced concrete design, TS 498 for the determination of structural loads, TS EN 206 and TS 13515 for concrete properties and conformity, and TS 708 for reinforcing steel. Technical rigour is maintained throughout structural analysis, material selection, construction methods and quality control, with structural integrity, safety, durability and long-term performance treated as core requirements.

Post-Handover Service Standards

Post-handover services form an integral part of Nova’s customer experience and long-term value approach. In line with its commitment to unconditional customer satisfaction, technical support, request management and resolution processes are managed within defined service standards. The objective is to maintain the same level of quality and trust beyond handover.
