Doğan Apartmanı

Erenköy, Bağdat Caddesi/İstanbul

The Project

Doğan Residence is a residential building in Erenköy, Bağdat Caddesi, completed in 2017. The project comprises twenty-four homes and a total construction area of 5,150 m².

The building has twelve residential floors above the ground floor and two basement levels, which include enclosed parking and building services.

Exterior Architecture

Architecture and façade details.

The architectural character of Doğan Residence is defined by a façade design that brings together composite cladding, contrasting surface textures, vertical architectural elements and carefully considered balcony details. The interplay of materials and colours reinforces the building’s architectural coherence, giving the façade a distinct identity.

Apartment Types

Twenty-four residences.

Building Systems

Comfort and building infrastructure.

The building includes two levels of underground parking with direct lift access to the residences, exterior thermal insulation and a swimming pool. A sound-insulated, fully automatic generator provides backup power during outages.
