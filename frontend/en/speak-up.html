Ethics & Compliance

Speak Up

NOVA KONUT İNŞAAT YATIRIM A.Ş. encourages employees, site personnel, contractors, suppliers, residents and neighbours to report serious concerns relating to its projects. Reports are assessed on the information provided. The principles and standards that guide our conduct are set out in our Ethical Principles & Labour Standards.

01 What to Report

Speak Up provides a channel for reporting serious concerns or suspected misconduct relating to NOVA’s activities, projects, employees, contractors and suppliers. This includes:

  • Unsafe conditions or practices on site or in the workplace.
  • Unlawful or unethical conduct.
  • Harassment or discrimination.
  • Coercion, forced labour or restrictions on a person’s freedom to leave employment.
  • Corruption, bribery or serious conflicts of interest.
  • Serious concerns involving contractors, subcontractors or suppliers.

Please provide a clear, factual account of your concern, including what happened, where and when it occurred, and the people or organisations involved. Specific information helps us assess a report; general allegations without sufficient detail may be difficult to investigate. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is not tolerated.

02 Your Identity

The form below does not request your name or contact details. If you would like NOVA to contact you for further information, you may provide a contact method in the optional field at the end of the form. Any information you enter there may identify you. Please include only the personal information you consider necessary in your description.

03 Emergencies

Speak Up is not an emergency service and is not monitored continuously. If there is an immediate risk to life, health or safety, or you suspect a crime is in progress, contact the relevant emergency services or public authorities first. In Türkiye, the emergency number is 112.
