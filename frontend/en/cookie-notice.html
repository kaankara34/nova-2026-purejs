Cookie Notice

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This notice explains the cookies, browser storage and third-party requests actually used on the NOVA Konut website, what each of them is for and how you can control them.

01 Scope

This notice explains the cookies, browser storage and third-party requests used on the website operated by NOVA KONUT İNŞAAT YATIRIM A.Ş. The Company’s registered electronic notification address (KEP) is novakonut@hs01.kep.tr.

Cookies are small data files that a website may store on your device through your browser. Browser local storage keeps information in the browser in a similar way. Loading content from another organisation’s servers — a font, a library, an image or a map — does not necessarily store anything on your device, but it does send connection data to that organisation.

02 What this website uses

This website sets no cookies of its own. It keeps two items in your browser’s local storage: one recording that the cookie notice has been dismissed, and one holding the most recent news file so that it does not have to be downloaded on every page view. It also loads a limited number of files and one embedded map from external providers in order to display its pages.

No analytics, advertising, profiling or social media tracking technology is used, and no technology requiring separate explicit consent has been identified. Because there is no optional technology to switch on or off, the website does not present an “Accept All” consent panel. The notice shown at the bottom of the page is informational; dismissing it records only that you have seen it and is not treated as consent to anything.

NOVA does not describe an external resource as necessary simply because it renders a font, map, image or animation. The purpose of each item is stated below, and requests that are not required are being reduced, including by serving static resources from NOVA’s own infrastructure where the licence permits.

04 Third parties and international transfers

Loading external content creates a direct connection between your device and the provider concerned, which transmits connection data such as your IP address, request time and browser information. Several of these providers operate their infrastructure outside Türkiye, so those requests involve a transfer of connection data abroad.

Transfers abroad are permitted only where a condition under Article 9 of Law No. 6698 applies. NOVA is reviewing these connections and the applicable conditions with its providers; this notice does not claim that a particular transfer condition or safeguard is currently in place, and it will be updated when the position is confirmed. Form content submitted through the website is not transmitted to these providers.

05 Managing your browser

Because the website uses no optional technologies requiring consent, there is no preference panel to configure. You can delete or block cookies, browser storage and third-party content at any time through your browser settings or an extension.

Clearing browser storage causes the cookie notice to be shown again and the news file to be downloaded on the next visit. Blocking external content may prevent the typefaces, news items or the location map from displaying correctly; the rest of the website continues to work.

06 Rights and contact

Requests concerning rights under Article 11 of Law No. 6698 may be submitted to NOVA KONUT İNŞAAT YATIRIM A.Ş. using the applicable request procedures. The Company’s KEP address is novakonut@hs01.kep.tr.
