Website Privacy Notice

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This notice sets out how NOVA KONUT İNŞAAT YATIRIM A.Ş. processes personal data through this website, the purposes and legal grounds for that processing, the categories of recipient involved and the rights available to individuals under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698.

01 Data controller and scope

This notice explains how NOVA KONUT İNŞAAT YATIRIM A.Ş. (“NOVA” or the “Company”) processes personal data through its website, in accordance with the information obligation under Article 10 of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“Law No. 6698”).

Data controller
NOVA KONUT İNŞAAT YATIRIM A.Ş.
Registered electronic notification address (KEP)
novakonut@hs01.kep.tr

The KEP address is a formal electronic notification channel used for legal notifications and for requests submitted through that channel.

This notice covers website visitors and individuals who contact the Company through the website. Separate notices apply to other relationships: job applicants are covered by the Applicant Privacy Notice, and reports submitted through Speak Up are covered by the information given on that page.

02 Data we process and how it is collected

Information you provide. The registration of interest and contact forms collect your name, telephone number and country dialling code, e-mail address, the project you are interested in, how you heard about NOVA, your preference as to receiving news and offers, and your confirmation that you have read this notice. The forms contain no free-text message field; any further information you include in correspondence you send us is also processed.

Technical information. When the website is requested, the infrastructure that delivers it may generate standard technical records such as an IP address, request time and browser or device information. The website does not set cookies of its own and does not use analytics, advertising or profiling technologies. A small amount of information is kept in your browser’s own storage so that the site can remember the cookie notice and display news without downloading the same file repeatedly; this is described in the Cookie Notice.

03 Purposes and legal grounds

Information submitted in an enquiry is processed to receive and respond to your request, provide project or service information you ask for, arrange meetings or visits where requested, and manage related correspondence.

Depending on the nature of the request, the applicable grounds under Article 5(2) of Law No. 6698 may include processing directly necessary for the establishment or performance of a contract, or the Company’s legitimate interests where these do not prejudice the individual’s fundamental rights and freedoms. Correspondence and related records may be processed for the establishment, exercise or protection of rights, and technical records to maintain the website’s operation and security, resolve errors and prevent misuse. Personal data is also processed where necessary to comply with a legal obligation.

Submitting an enquiry does not by itself constitute consent to receive advertising or promotional electronic messages. The news and offers preference is a separate, optional choice and such communications remain subject to the applicable consent and opt-out requirements.

04 Recipients

Personal data is shared only to the extent necessary for the purposes above, with the following categories of recipient:

  • information technology and communication service providers that operate the website, its hosting, e-mail and form infrastructure;
  • professional advisers and service providers acting on NOVA’s behalf in relation to an enquiry;
  • competent public authorities and courts, where disclosure is legally required.

Enquiries submitted through the website are directed to the Company’s enquiry mailbox at iletisim@nova.istanbul. NOVA does not sell personal data and does not share it for third-party advertising purposes.

05 International transfers

Some of the technical services used to deliver this website — for example content delivery for typefaces, interface libraries, the static news file and the embedded location map — are operated by providers with infrastructure outside Türkiye. When your browser requests that content, connection data such as your IP address, request time and browser information reaches the relevant provider abroad. This is limited to connection data: enquiry content submitted through the forms is not sent to those providers.

Whether any further transfer abroad takes place through the Company’s hosting, e-mail or form infrastructure depends on where those services are operated. NOVA is verifying this with its providers.

Transfers of personal data abroad are permitted only where a condition set out in Article 9 of Law No. 6698 applies. This notice does not assert that a particular transfer condition or safeguard is currently in place; the position is under review, and this notice will be updated once it is confirmed. Reducing avoidable third-party requests, including serving static resources from NOVA’s own infrastructure, is part of that review.

06 Retention and disposal

Personal data is retained for the period necessary for its processing purpose and applicable legal obligations. The nature of the enquiry, any continuing legal relationship and periods relevant to potential legal claims are taken into account. When the grounds for processing and retention cease to apply, the data is deleted, destroyed or anonymised in accordance with applicable law. Specific retention periods are set out in the Company’s internal retention schedule.

07 Your rights

Subject to Article 11 of Law No. 6698, you may ask whether your personal data is processed, request information about its processing, learn its purpose and recipients, request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, request deletion or destruction where the statutory conditions are met, and exercise your other rights under the Law.

Requests may be submitted to NOVA KONUT İNŞAAT YATIRIM A.Ş. in accordance with the applicable request procedures; the Company’s KEP address for submissions through that channel is novakonut@hs01.kep.tr. Requests are assessed within the applicable statutory period.
