A home is more than a place to live.

It shapes the way everyday life feels.

Scroll to explore

Home is only the beginning.

A well-designed home can change how the day begins. Living well extends beyond its rooms.

It is the comfort of feeling at ease at home. Knowing who to contact and where to turn when you need support. Having places close at hand to work, unwind and spend time with family and friends. Taking care of everyday tasks without interrupting your day.

Nova considers the home, shared spaces and resident services together.

Direct support for everyday needs.
Smooth, uninterrupted service.

Everyday life, carefully considered

Making life easier should be simple.

A technical issue at home.
A restaurant reservation.
A guest arriving.
A shared space to reserve.
A question awaiting an answer.

These are everyday situations.

What matters is how easily they are resolved.

The Nova Membership app gives residents a single point of access for all these needs.

At Nova, service is part of the residential experience from the day you move in.
