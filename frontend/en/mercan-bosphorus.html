Mercan Bosphorus

ÇENGELKÖY | İSTANBUL

Set in Çengelköy, a Bosphorus neighbourhood known for its historic waterfront and enduring local character, Mercan Bosphorus is a residential development distinguished by its Bosphorus views and considered architecture. Reflecting the character of its surroundings, the development brings together landscaped grounds, residents’ social spaces, and facilities for fitness and relaxation.

A Complete World by the Bosphorus

Key Features

Beyond its residences, Mercan Bosphorus offers an extensive range of social, sports and wellness amenities designed around everyday life. Indoor and outdoor swimming pools, a fitness center, boxing ring, a basketball court, sauna, steam room and Turkish bath are complemented by landscaped gardens and a preserved historic landmark within the grounds. With 24/7 security, underground parking, private storage and full-capacity backup power, the development combines comfort, recreation and functionality within a secure and thoughtfully planned residential setting.

Bosphorus Views

Residences enjoy direct views across the Bosphorus, bringing one of Istanbul’s most defining landscapes into everyday life from the distinctive setting of Çengelköy.

Wellness & Recreation

From indoor and outdoor swimming pools to fitness facilities, a boxing ring, sauna, steam room and traditional Turkish bath, Mercan Bosphorus offers an extensive wellness environment designed for both active living and relaxation.

Landscaped Gardens

Thoughtfully designed landscaped gardens create a calm and private setting throughout Mercan Bosphorus. Lush planting, mature greenery and carefully arranged outdoor spaces bring nature into everyday life, offering residents a peaceful retreat within the city.
