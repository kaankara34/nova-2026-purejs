Martı Residence

SUADİYE, BAĞDAT CADDESİ | İSTANBUL

Set on Noter Street, one of the most prestigious addresses off Bağdat Avenue, Martı Residence is a signature NOVA development offering panoramic sea views and exceptional sunset vistas. Its unobstructed outlook is preserved by the surrounding planning regulations, while premium amenities such as an outdoor swimming pool and secure underground parking complete the living experience.

KEY FEATURES

Martı Residence offers unobstructed panoramic sea views from one of the most desirable locations on the Bağdat Avenue coastline. Ideally situated between Bağdat Avenue and the waterfront, it combines the energy of the city with the tranquility of the sea. Exclusive amenities include a private outdoor swimming pool and secure underground parking, delivering a refined residential experience.

Panoramic Sea Views

Experience panoramic sea views from one of Suadiye's most privileged waterfront locations, just moments from both Bağdat Avenue and the coastline.

Private Outdoor Pool

Designed for everyday comfort, the private outdoor pool provides an exclusive setting for relaxation in a quiet, landscaped environment.

Prime Location

Located on prestigious Noter Street in Suadiye, Martı Residence enjoys one of the most sought-after addresses between Bağdat Avenue and the Marmara coastline, offering effortless access to both city life and the sea.
